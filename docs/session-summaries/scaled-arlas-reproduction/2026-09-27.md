1. **Keep Qwen3-4B as the provisional model.** It passes the frozen benign
   competence and tool-validity gates and shows nonzero static susceptibility.
2. **Do not rerun old GPU generations solely to reproduce their numbers.** Raw
   traces are complete enough to regenerate derived reports. New inference is
   justified only by repaired measurement or the new suite-selection protocol.
3. **Treat protocol v0.3 as authoritative.** The dated paper plan is superseded
   design history where the two conflict.
4. **Separate feasibility from publishable evidence.** A promising ASR does not
   override verifier-integrity, capability, utility, or completeness failures.
5. **Resolve inference and budget issues before training.** Post-outcome changes
   to SESOI, endpoints, split rules, or ceilings would damage credibility.
